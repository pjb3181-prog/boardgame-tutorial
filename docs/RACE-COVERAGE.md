## v0.10.0 — beginner pedagogy and continuous example
- Complete beginners are the acceptance audience. Teach vocabulary and the three card roles before physical setup. Explain how to identify numbered start worlds vs unboxed preset-hand numbers, and demonstrate inspecting/discarding a six-card hand. Four rounds use the same fictional hand/tableau: end states (hand, tableau, goods, VP chips) are (3,2,0,0), (2,3,1,0), (4,3,1,0), (5,3,0,2). Other players' decisions are scripted, not fully simulated; standard single-action selection is taught before experienced 2p. The three optional questions follow the demonstrations. A physical setup and first-round checklist ends the guide. Keep all existing expansion lessons.
